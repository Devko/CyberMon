Navigate

Hover a group to see its names by scheme; click to pin it. Hover a hub, or press a scheme above, to highlight the groups it names. Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom.

With the graph focused, arrow keys pan, +/− zoom, Home resets, ] and [ pin the next and previous group, and Esc clears the pin. Browse groups lists the same groups as a table.

The page address keeps the pinned group, the highlighted scheme, the search and the options above.

Threat groups × naming schemes

A group with names in one scheme sits beside that hub; a group with names in several sits between them, and the groups with the most schemes sit near the middle. ATT&CK lists these names under one group, but each vendor's cluster can overlap the others without being the same group.
